REALITY CHECK — EXPLAINED

What this shows

Compares what the math predicts you'd lose against what your scale actually showed over the last 14 days. The gap reveals whether your TDEE estimate is accurate.

Predicted vs Actual loss

Predicted = (Formula TDEE − average intake) × span ÷ 7700. Actual = scale weight at start − scale weight today. A gap of −40% means you lost 40% less than predicted; −15% to +15% is normal noise; beyond that suggests your real burn rate differs from formula.

Eating-day avg vs Period avg

Eating-day avg = calories you eat on days you actually eat. Period avg includes fast days (counted as 0 cal), pulling the number down. Both are correct — they answer different questions. Use eating-day avg to gauge your meal sizes; period avg drives the TDEE math.

Formula vs Observed TDEE

Formula TDEE = Mifflin-St Jeor × your activity multiplier (set during onboarding). Observed TDEE = derived from your actual intake + actual weight change using CICO. They differ when your real activity drops below the multiplier (sick days, travel, low-energy weeks) or when adaptive thermogenesis kicks in on a sustained deficit.

When calibration applies

Auto-calibration runs once per 7 days. It applies a 70/30 blend of observed/formula only if the gap is greater than 7% AND the observed value is within physiologically safe bounds (above your BMR, below formula × 1.5). Otherwise it skips and tries again next cycle.

What you can do

Tap RESET in Settings if TDEE drifts wrong. Use the FREEZE TDEE toggle to stop auto-calibration and pin your number. Sickness/water-spike protection is built in (Phase 7+) to keep the math honest during disrupted weeks.
