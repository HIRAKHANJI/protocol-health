EVIDENCE LAYER
SCIENCE-BACKED, NOT VIBES
Every supplement dose, macro target, and training ratio in this app is checked against published research before it ships. The codebase includes a mandatory science reference directive that blocks unsafe recommendations.
🧬
AUTO TDEE — MIFFLIN-ST JEOR
TDEE auto-calculated from the Mifflin-St Jeor equation (10 × weight + 6.25 × height − 5 × age ± offset) × activity multiplier. The most validated clinical BMR equation for overweight and obese individuals.
Mifflin et al. 1990 · AJCN · Validated across 498 subjects
⚖️
PUSH:PULL RATIO RULE
The app never generates a plan with push:pull ratio above 1:1. Default for aggressive plans is 5:7 pull-dominant. Prevents anterior shoulder dominance and impingement under handstand/planche loading.
Cools 2016 (scapular stabilisers) · Prinold 2016 (pull-up kinematics) · PMC
🛡️
ZINC SAFETY ENFORCEMENT
Zinc dosing is hard-coded as alternate-day only. 50mg × 4 days/week = 28.6mg/day average — within the NIH 40mg UL. Daily dosing would block copper absorption and cause deficiency anemia. The app enforces this rule.
NIH ODS Zinc Fact Sheet · UL = 40mg/day average
📊
ODS / NIH FACT SHEETS
Every supplement dose in the app is checked against the relevant ODS fact sheet before shipping. Hard-coded limits: D3 ≤ 4,000 IU, Mg (supplemental) ≤ 350mg, Potassium OTC ≤ 99mg/serving, MCT ≤ 30g acute.
ods.od.nih.gov · All nutrient fact sheets tier-1 referenced
🏋️
CALISTHENICS EVIDENCE
Push-up progressions produce equivalent strength gains to bench press (Kotarsky 2018). Rep-based progression matches load-based for muscle growth (Plotkin 2022). The repetition continuum supports high-rep bodyweight work (Schoenfeld 2021).
PubMed: 29466268 · PMC: 9528903 · PMC: 7927075
📐
ISSN POSITION STANDS
Protein targets (1.3–1.6g/kg during cut) and macro splits follow ISSN position stands on diets and body composition, protein and exercise, and the 2018 master review of sports nutrition.
ISSN 2017 · PMC: 5470183 · PMC: 6090881 · PubMed: 28642676
HOW IT'S BUILT
ZERO BLOAT ARCHITECTURE
One file. No backend. No framework. No subscriptions. Every design decision optimises for the person using it.
📱
INSTALLABLE PWA
Install on Android Chrome or iOS Safari (home screen) as a fullscreen app. Own icon, no browser chrome, full offline capability after first load.
🔒
YOUR DATA, YOUR DEVICE
Everything stored in localStorage. No server, no accounts, no cloud sync. Backup/restore to JSON file. Your data never leaves your phone.
⚡
ZERO DEPENDENCIES
Single HTML file. No npm, no build process, no bundler. Google Fonts is the only external request. Everything else is self-contained.
🌙
DARK THEME ONLY
Designed for phone use. Dark background, high-contrast accents, monospace data displays. No light mode because you don't need one.
💪
BODYWEIGHT FIRST
Zero equipment, zero location dependency. Every workout defaults to calisthenics. CARs explained, not assumed. Train anywhere.
📊
SMART PROJECTION
Weight projection blends observed rate with deficit math. Accounts for fast days, light days, compliance rate, and actual food intake data.
