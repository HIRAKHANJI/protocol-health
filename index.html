HOW IT'S BUILT
ZERO BLOAT ARCHITECTURE
One file. No backend. No framework. No subscriptions. Every design decision optimises for the person using it.
📱
INSTALLABLE PWA
Install on Android Chrome as a fullscreen app. Own icon, no browser chrome, full offline capability after first load.
🔒
YOUR DATA, YOUR DEVICE
Everything stored in localStorage. No server, no accounts, no cloud sync. Your weight, your food, your notes — never leave your phone.
⚡
ZERO DEPENDENCIES
Single HTML file. No npm, no build process, no bundler. Google Fonts is the only external request. Everything else is self-contained.
🌙
DARK THEME ONLY
Designed for phone use. Dark background, high-contrast accents, monospace data displays. No light mode because you don't need one.
💪
BODYWEIGHT FIRST
Zero equipment, zero location dependency. Every workout defaults to calisthenics. Train anywhere — hotel room, park, your living room.
📊
SMART PROJECTION
Weight projection blends observed rate with deficit math. Accounts for fast days, exercise burn, compliance rate. Not just TDEE minus calories.
