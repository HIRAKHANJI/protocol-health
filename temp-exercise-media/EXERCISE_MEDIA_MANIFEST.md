## Recommended Free/Open Sources

### Best Options (actually free to use):
1. **wger.de** - Open source fitness tracker, images under various licenses
   - API: https://wger.de/api/v2/exerciseimage/?format=json
   - Browse: https://wger.de/en/exercise/overview/
   - License: Check per-image, many are CC-BY-SA

2. **Wikimedia Commons** - Search "bodyweight exercise" category
   - https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Bodyweight_exercises
   - License: Varies per image, check each one

3. **Create your own** - Record short clips and convert to GIF
   - Most reliable for exact exercises in your progressions
   - Tools: Phone camera + ezgif.com for conversion

### Sources that look free but are NOT:
- musclewiki.com - Proprietary GIFs, not licensed for redistribution
- Most fitness blogs - Stock photos or proprietary content
- Pinterest/Instagram - Never free to use

---

## File Naming Convention

- Progressions: `{group}-{level}.gif` (e.g., `push-2.gif`)
- Non-progression: `{exercise-kebab-case}.gif` (e.g., `mountain-climbers.gif`)
- Target: Under 500KB each, animated GIF preferred
